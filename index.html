Five-bar leg playground

Set the two servo angles, build a loop of poses, and watch the foot trace its path. Matches your sketch: each servo’s 90° points outward, the left servo hangs down at 180°, and the right servo hangs down at 0°.

Dimensions

Any unit works. Joint length is the servo horn to the elbow; lower link is the elbow to the foot. Servo spacing is measured pivot to pivot. Servo length only changes the drawing. The links can only swing on the outer side of each servo, as in your sketch.

Animation

Left °Right °Time ms

Each row is the pose the leg moves to, and the time is how long that move takes. The last row blends back into the first when looping.
